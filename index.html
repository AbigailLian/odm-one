Triage classification criteria

Applied by the screening officer on receipt, before any document reaches the Deputy Minister's desk.

Red — Immediate Decision

SLA: DM decision within 24 hours
  • Deadline falls within 48 hours of receipt
  • Requires the DM's signature or a decision only the DM can make
  • Involves external agencies (TESDA, CSC, DBM, OCM) with fixed response dates
  • Legal, audit, or compliance exposure if unanswered

Yellow — Decision This Week

SLA: DM decision within 5 working days
  • Deadline within 5 working days
  • Designations, endorsements, and representation requests
  • Routine approvals that still require DM action

Green — For Information

SLA: acknowledge and close within 1 working day
  • Advisories, circulars, newsletters — no action required of the DM
  • Filed with control number for the record, then closed

Blue — For Delegation

SLA: route to office concerned within 1 working day
  • Action required, but within an office's delegated authority
  • Routed directly to the office concerned; DM sees only the weekly summary
  • Governed by the ODM delegation of authority matrix

Intake rule

No document reaches the Deputy Minister's desk without (1) a control number, (2) a completed Category and Deadline field, and (3) for Red and Yellow items, a one-page CSW Annex B stating the recommended action. The DM decides; the staff work is done before the folder opens.

Weekly review

Every Friday, a 15-minute review with the DM or Executive Assistant covering: overdue items, items awaiting DM decision for more than 3 days, and the count completed. Tracked metric: average days from receipt to DM decision.
